CE 353 Quiz: get your phone ready

Do this before class. It takes two minutes.

1. Allow location

The quiz opens only for students who are in the lecture room, so your phone must be allowed to share its location with this site.

This test shows you the result only. Nothing is sent to the lecturer.

2. Sign in with your KNUST account

The quiz forms accept only your KNUST school email (ending in knust.edu.gh). Sign in now and tick Stay signed in if you are asked.

Open Microsoft Forms and sign in

You should see a page that says Microsoft Forms with your name at the top right. If it says your account is not allowed, see the lecturer before the quiz.

3. On quiz day

  • Use the same phone and the same browser (Chrome or Safari) that you used here.
  • Do not use private or incognito mode.
  • Charge your phone and turn on mobile data or Wi-Fi.
  • Know your index number. You will type it, your full name, and the room code from the screen.
  • Scan the QR code on the lecture-room screen. A QR code from a friend or from a photo will not work.
  • You get 10 minutes. The questions come one at a time and you cannot go back.
