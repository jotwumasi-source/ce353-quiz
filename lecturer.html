Enter the admin key and press Load

1. Quiz

"Start a NEW quiz" stops every old card and closes entry (you must type NEW to confirm). Generating cards opens entry automatically. Students already inside can always finish.

2. Cards

Every card gets its own random parameters, a 12-character card ID and an 8-digit one-time access code. The codes are shown only once, right after generating: print or download them now.
